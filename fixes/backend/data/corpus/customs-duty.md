# How import duty is built up

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Import cost is built up from the assessable value (usually the CIF value) plus basic customs duty (BCD), a social welfare surcharge calculated on the BCD where applicable, and IGST charged on the combined value. The rates come from the tariff schedule and depend on the HS code; exemption notifications and free trade agreements such as the India-Japan CEPA can reduce duty for eligible goods with proper origin proof. Classification disputes are a common cause of delay, so pre-classify products and keep technical documentation ready.
