# Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

The central government runs PLI schemes that pay incentives on incremental sales of goods made in India across a range of sectors, including automobiles and auto components, electronics and others. Each scheme has its own eligibility criteria, minimum investment and sales thresholds, and application windows. They mainly benefit larger investments, so check whether a smaller entrant can qualify before including them in a business case.
