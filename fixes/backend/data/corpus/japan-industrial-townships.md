# Japan-focused industrial areas in India

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Several Indian states host industrial areas that market themselves to Japanese firms, with dedicated support desks, ready plots and utilities. Reported examples include the Japanese zone near Neemrana in Rajasthan and Japan-oriented areas in Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh. Japanese automakers and their supplier networks cluster around Gurugram-Manesar (Haryana), Gujarat, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu. JETRO and the Embassy of Japan keep current lists of Japanese companies and desks; confirm the details before naming a location.
