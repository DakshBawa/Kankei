# India-Japan trade and tax agreements

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

India and Japan have a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA, in force since 2011) that reduces or removes tariffs on many goods when origin rules are met, and a bilateral double taxation avoidance agreement (DTAA) that sets maximum withholding rates on dividends, interest and royalties and defines permanent establishment rules. The exact tariff lines and treaty rates should be read from the current texts before they are used in a landed-cost or structuring decision.
