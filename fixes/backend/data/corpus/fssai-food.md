# Food products (FSSAI)

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Food businesses, including importers of food products, need FSSAI registration or a licence, and imported food is subject to clearance and labelling requirements. Labels must carry specified information in prescribed formats. Check product-specific standards before shipping a first consignment.
