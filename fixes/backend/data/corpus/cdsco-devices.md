# Medical device regulation (CDSCO)

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Medical devices are regulated under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017, with devices grouped into risk classes A to D. Importers generally need an import licence and manufacturers need registration or licensing according to the device class. Documentation such as a quality management system certificate, a device master file and free-sale certificates from the country of origin is typically required. Timelines vary with the class.
