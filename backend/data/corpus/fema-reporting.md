# FEMA and RBI reporting for foreign investment

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

The Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) governs cross-border investment and payments. Inbound investment has to be reported to the RBI through its online reporting system within prescribed timelines, using forms such as FC-GPR for the issue of shares. Indian companies with foreign investment also file an annual return on foreign liabilities and assets. Liaison and branch offices file annual activity certificates. Missing a deadline can lead to late-submission fees, so build these dates into the compliance roadmap.
