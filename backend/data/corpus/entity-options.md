# Entity options for a foreign company entering India

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Common structures: a wholly owned subsidiary (usually a private limited company), a joint venture with an Indian partner, a limited liability partnership (LLP, available where FDI rules allow), and a branch, liaison or project office. A liaison office is limited to liaison and representative activities and cannot earn income in India. Branch and liaison offices need RBI approval through an authorised dealer bank. A subsidiary gives the most operational freedom and limited liability, but requires incorporation, capital and ongoing compliance. A JV adds local knowledge and relationships, but needs a carefully drafted shareholders' agreement.
