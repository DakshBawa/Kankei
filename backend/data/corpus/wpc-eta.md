# Wireless equipment approval (WPC / ETA)

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Products with radio or wireless functions, such as Bluetooth or Wi-Fi modules, generally need an equipment type approval from the telecom authorities before import and sale. Industrial sensors with wireless connectivity may fall under this. Check the current procedure and the frequency bands that are covered, as the process and online portals have changed over time.
