# State industrial policies and incentives

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Indian states compete for manufacturing investment with their own industrial policies. Typical incentives include capital subsidies, reimbursement of state GST, stamp duty exemptions, power tariff concessions and subsidised land in industrial parks. Eligibility depends on investment size, sector, location (for example backward districts) and the date of the policy. Compare the current policy documents of each shortlisted state and apply before starting commercial production where required.
