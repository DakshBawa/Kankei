# Transfer pricing for related-party transactions

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Transactions between a Japanese parent and its Indian subsidiary or JV are treated as international transactions between associated enterprises. They must be priced at arm's length and documented, and a report from a chartered accountant is filed with the tax return when thresholds are met. Intra-group royalties, management fees and the price of goods supplied to the Indian entity are the usual areas of scrutiny.
