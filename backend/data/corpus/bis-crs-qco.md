# BIS certification: Compulsory Registration and Quality Control Orders

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Some product categories must be certified or registered with BIS before they can be manufactured, imported or sold in India. This happens through the Compulsory Registration Scheme for listed electronics and IT goods and through Quality Control Orders for other categories. Whether an industrial sensor falls under a mandatory scheme depends on the exact product and its standard, so check product by product. Foreign manufacturers usually need testing in a recognised laboratory and may need to appoint an Indian authorised representative.
