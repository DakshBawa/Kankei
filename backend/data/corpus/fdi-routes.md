# FDI routes into India: automatic vs government approval

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Foreign direct investment enters India through two routes. Under the automatic route no prior government approval is needed. Under the government route the investment needs prior approval. Which route applies depends on the sector, and some sectors have caps or conditions (for example on ownership percentage or local sourcing). Manufacturing of most industrial goods is generally open, but the consolidated FDI policy must be checked for the specific sector and product. After investment, the Indian company reports the inflow to the RBI.
