# GST registration and basics for foreign-owned businesses

_Starter summary written from general knowledge, not quoted from the source. Verify against the official source before relying on it._

Goods and Services Tax is levied on supplies of goods and services. Registration is required once turnover crosses thresholds that vary by type of supply and state, and some businesses must register regardless of turnover. A registered business can generally claim input tax credit on GST paid on business purchases. Imports attract Integrated GST (IGST) at the border. Rates depend on the HS classification of the product, so classification should be settled early.
